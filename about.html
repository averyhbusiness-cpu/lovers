About

The site
never gets
built

Every owner means to get around to it. Then a supplier calls, somebody quits, the truck needs brakes, and it goes back on the pile. A year later the business is still running on a phone number and word of mouth, and the customers who went looking and found nothing never mention it. That is the entire problem. It is not urgent, so it stays undone.

How the work is checked

A cold call from somebody you have never heard of is a bad way to meet, and being told to trust a stranger is worse. So none of this runs on trust.

  • The domain is registered in your name. You own it, not me. If you ever want to walk, the site and the address go with you and I cannot hold either one.
  • You see it before you pay the balance. The site gets built and shown to you first. If it is wrong, it gets changed before any of it goes live.
  • Two prices, and you hear both up front. One fee to build it, one monthly to host it and keep it updated. No third charge appears later.
  • The monthly can be cancelled. No contract and no term. If you stop, you stop, and you take the domain with you.
  • Stripe handles the money. Your card details go to them and never to me. You get a receipt by email the moment it goes through.
  • A real phone number that a real person answers. It is on every page of this site. Call it and find out.

Why it costs less than an agency

The quote you get from a web design firm covers a lot of things that never touch your website.

  • No sales team Agencies pay somebody to take the first call, somebody else to write the proposal, and both of them get paid out of your quote whether you sign or not.
  • No account manager in the middle You are talking to the person building the site. Nothing gets relayed through a third person, which is where most of the cost and most of the mistakes come from.
  • No office There is no rent baked into the number. The monthly you pay covers hosting and the changes you ask for, and nothing else.
  • No advertising budget This is why you got a phone call instead of a sponsored ad. Finding customers costs me time rather than money, and that difference ends up in the price.

The work itself is not cheaper. It is the same building, done by the same hands, usually better for the simple reason that it does not get handed between three people before it reaches you. What is cheaper is everything wrapped around it.

What this is not

This is not a marketing service. There is no strategy to buy, no funnel, no ad spend to manage. Nobody here guarantees rankings, because anybody guaranteeing rankings is guessing.

The scope is narrow on purpose. A site gets built that says who you are, what you do and how to reach you, and then it stays correct. That is the whole offer. If you do not want one, that is the end of it and you will not get called about it again.
